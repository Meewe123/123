# Privacy Policy — Orbital Rush

**Last updated: 10 September 2026**

## The short version

Orbital Rush collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no
advertising, no third-party SDK, and no network request of any kind. The app
works with the device permanently in airplane mode.

## What is stored, and where

The app saves one thing: your progress. That is your best score, your best
chain, total rings passed, energy balance, which trails you have unlocked, your
daily missions and streak, and your sound/haptics settings.

This is written to the app's own private storage on your device (browser
`localStorage` inside the app's WebView, mirrored to the system preference
store). It never leaves the device. It is not readable by other apps. It is not
backed up to any server operated by us — if your device backup includes app
data, that copy is governed by Apple's iCloud terms, not by us.

Deleting the app deletes this data. "Reset progress" in Settings erases it
immediately without deleting the app.

## What is not collected

* No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
* No advertising identifier (IDFA), and no request for tracking permission.
* No device identifiers, IP address logging, crash reporting or analytics.
* No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
* No purchase data — the app contains no in-app purchases.

## Third parties

There are none. Orbital Rush contains no advertising network, no analytics
provider, no social login and no cloud service. The entire app — including its
sound, which is synthesised at runtime rather than streamed — ships inside the
binary.

## Children

Orbital Rush is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because it collects no data
at all, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from
children under 13, and it complies with COPPA and with Apple's Kids Category
requirements by simply having nothing to collect.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, the CCPA and comparable laws you have rights to access,
correct, export and erase personal data held about you. We hold none, so there
is nothing to access or erase on our side. The copy on your device is entirely
under your control via "Reset progress" or by deleting the app.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated
before that version ships, and the change will be described in the release
notes.
